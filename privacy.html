Water Signal Tracker™

Privacy Policy · Effective September 7, 2026

Water Signal Tracker™ is operated by SYGNL Technologies™. Water Signal Tracker helps communities report and understand water-service conditions, including outages, weak pressure, intermittent service, and restoration status.

Information We Collect

Water Signal Tracker may collect information that users choose to provide when submitting a report, including:

  • Water-service condition
  • General location or parish
  • Date and time of a report
  • Optional comments or service details
  • Device or technical information needed for the app to function
  • Location information, if the user grants permission for location-based features

We do not require users to provide medical information, biometric information, or sensitive health information.

How We Use Information

Information collected through Water Signal Tracker may be used to:

  • Display community water-service conditions
  • Identify patterns in outages, pressure, intermittent service, and restoration
  • Improve the accuracy and usefulness of the platform
  • Generate aggregated community water-service insights
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the application
  • Support research, resilience planning, and infrastructure intelligence

Community Reports

Information submitted through Water Signal Tracker may contribute to aggregated community-level statistics, maps, timelines, and service-condition reports. We aim to present community information without unnecessarily exposing personally identifying information.

Location Information

If location access is enabled, Water Signal Tracker may use device location to help associate a report with the correct geographic area. Users may decline location permission and may change location permissions through their device settings.

Sharing of Information

SYGNL Technologies does not sell personal information.

We may share aggregated or de-identified water-service information with utilities, researchers, government agencies, infrastructure stakeholders, resilience organizations, or other partners where appropriate.

Personal information may also be disclosed if required by law or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information collected through Water Signal Tracker. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Water Signal Tracker, maintain historical water-service records, support analysis, comply with legal obligations, or improve the platform.

Children

Water Signal Tracker is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-Party Services

Water Signal Tracker may rely on third-party infrastructure or technology providers to operate certain features. Those providers may process limited technical information according to their own privacy practices.

Your Choices

Users may:

  • Decline location permissions
  • Adjust device permissions
  • Contact SYGNL Technologies with questions about their information
  • Request correction or deletion of personally identifiable information where applicable

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Water Signal Tracker develops. The effective date at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.
